Culture

More than a song

Deuda is not simply music. It is a living tradition of Sudurpaschim and Karnali — carried through circles, voices, stories, rhythm, and generations.

Memory

Songs that travelled by voice

Before playlists and streaming, these melodies travelled from village to village, from one chautari to another, remembered and sung by people.

Together

Music brings people closer

Whether you grew up in the hills or now live far from home, an old deuda tune can bring back the sound of familiar places and familiar voices.
